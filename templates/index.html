📊 About the Project

This system uses Natural Language Processing to analyze textual feedback and classify it into Positive, Negative, or Neutral sentiment. It also identifies frequent words and sentiment trends.

🛒 Real-World Example: Wireless Earphones

Rating: ⭐ 3.9 / 5

Review: "Sound quality is excellent and I am very happy."

System Result: Positive


A numerical rating tells us what rating the customer gave, while sentiment analysis helps understand why the customer feels that way.
